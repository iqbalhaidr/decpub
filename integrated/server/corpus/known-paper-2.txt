Incentive mechanisms for volunteer reviewers usually reward speed alone, which encourages short and superficial reports. We propose a hybrid weighting scheme that combines the subjective preferences of the journal with objective statistics computed from the reviews themselves. The subjective weights come from the best worst method, while the objective weights combine entropy and inter criteria correlation. A normalised distance to the ideal solution then ranks the reviewers, and a smooth saturating function converts the ranking into a share of the fee paid by the author. Simulation of free riders and of newly created identities shows that the scheme pays careful reviewers more than careless ones without requiring any editor to assign the weights by hand.
